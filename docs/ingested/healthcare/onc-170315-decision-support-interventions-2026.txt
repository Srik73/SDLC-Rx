(11) Decision support interventions —

(i) Decision support intervention interaction. Interventions provided to a user must occur when a user is interacting with technology.

(ii) Decision support configuration.
(A) Enable interventions specified in paragraph (b)(11)(iii) of this section to be configured by a limited set of identified users based on a user's role.
(B) Enable interventions when a patient's medications, allergies and intolerance, and problems are incorporated from a transition of care or referral summary received and pursuant to paragraph (b)(2)(iii)(D) of this section.
(C) Enable a user to provide electronic feedback data for evidence-based decision support interventions selected via the capability provided in paragraph (b)(11)(iii)(A) of this section and make available such feedback data to a limited set of identified users for export, in a computable format, including at a minimum the intervention, action taken, user feedback provided (if applicable), user, date, and location.

(iii) Decision support intervention selection. Enable a limited set of identified users to select (i.e., activate) electronic decision support interventions (in addition to drug-drug and drug-allergy contraindication checking) that are:
(A) Evidence-based decision support interventions using data on: problems; medications; allergies and intolerances; at least one demographic specified in paragraph (a)(5)(i); laboratory; vital signs; unique device identifier(s) for a patient's implantable device(s); and procedures.
(B) Predictive Decision Support Interventions and use any data expressed in the standards in § 170.213.

(iv) Source attributes — required for both evidence-based and Predictive Decision Support Interventions, including:
(A) For evidence-based interventions: bibliographic citation; developer; funding source; release/revision dates; and use of race, ethnicity, language, sexual orientation, gender identity, sex, date of birth, social determinants of health data, and health status assessment data as input.
(B) For Predictive Decision Support Interventions — significantly more extensive disclosure requirements, including:
  (1) Details and output: developer name/contact, funding source, description of value produced, and whether output is a prediction, classification, recommendation, evaluation, or analysis.
  (2) Purpose: intended use, intended patient population(s), intended user(s), intended decision-making role (informs, augments, replaces clinical management).
  (3) Cautioned out-of-scope use: tasks/situations/populations where use is cautioned against; known risks, inappropriate settings, inappropriate uses, or known limitations.
  (4) Intervention development details and input features: exclusion/inclusion criteria for training data; use of demographic variables as input features; description of demographic representativeness of training data; description of relevance of training data to intended deployed setting.
  (5) Process used to ensure fairness: approach taken to ensure fair output; approaches to manage, reduce, or eliminate bias.
  (6) External validation process: data source/clinical setting where validity and fairness were assessed (other than training data); party that conducted external testing; demographic representativeness of external data; description of external validation process.
  (7) Quantitative measures of performance: validity and fairness in same-source test data; validity and fairness in external/different-source data; references to evaluation of intervention's effect on outcomes (morbidity, mortality, length of stay, etc.).
  (8) Ongoing maintenance: process/frequency of validity monitoring over time; validity in local data; process/frequency of fairness monitoring over time; fairness in local data.
  (9) Update schedule: process/frequency of intervention updates; process/frequency of performance correction when validity/fairness risks are identified.

(v) Source attribute access and modification.
(A) Access — a limited set of identified users must be able to access complete, up-to-date, plain-language descriptions of source attribute information. For Predictive Decision Support Interventions, the technology must indicate when certain attribute information is not available for review.
(B) Modify — a limited set of identified users must be able to record, change, and access source attributes, and (for Predictive DSIs) additional source attributes not otherwise specified.

(vi) Intervention risk management. Risk management practices must be applied to each Predictive Decision Support Intervention supplied by the developer:
(A) Risk analysis — analysis of potential risks and adverse impacts related to: validity, reliability, robustness, fairness, intelligibility, safety, security, and privacy.
(B) Risk mitigation — practices to mitigate risks identified under (A).
(C) Governance — policies and implemented controls for governance, including how data are acquired, managed, and used.
